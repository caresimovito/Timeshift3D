# Chronoshift — Era One trailer script (for Google Flow)

Runtime 44.5 s. Nine beats. Flow generates in short clips, so each beat below is written to
stand alone as one prompt; the narration line for that beat goes in the same clip.

Visual descriptions are what is actually in the level, not invention — same geography, plants
and creatures as the in-engine cut.

---

## 1 — ARRIVAL JUNGLE · 0:00–0:05

**Visual:** Dense prehistoric jungle at low sun. Enormous gnarled broadleaf trees with thick
buttress roots, tree ferns and cycads crowding a narrow game trail. Shafts of light through
haze, drifting motes. Camera pushes slowly forward along the trail.

**Narration:** "Chronoshift."
**Then:** "A time traveller, stranded a hundred million years before his own."

---

## 2 — GRAZING MEADOW · 0:05–0:10

**Visual:** The jungle opens into a wide dry valley. Tall araucaria conifers and tree ferns
scattered across yellow-green grass, red sandstone bluffs either side. A brontosaurus grazes
in the middle distance. Camera drifts out across the open ground.

**Narration:** "Era One. The prehistoric age."

---

## 3 — THE POND · 0:10–0:14

**Visual:** A still mossy pond in a shallow bowl, lily pads on dark water, reeds and horsetails
at the margin, soft reflected light. Camera glides low over the water toward the far bank.

**Narration:** "You arrive with nothing. You survive with a spear, a club, a sling and a bola."

---

## 4 — SWAMP MARSH · 0:14–0:18

**Visual:** Flooded cypress swamp, pale trunks standing in shallow green water, hanging moss,
mist low across the surface. A faint bioluminescent glow from a clutch of eggs on a bank.

**Narration:** *(continues over)*

---

## 5 — NESTING GROUNDS · 0:18–0:25

**Visual:** A deep green mossy valley between banded sandstone cliffs. A natural stone arch
spans the middle distance. Scattered dinosaur bones and ribcages across the valley floor,
fallen mossy logs and exposed root clusters. Raptors move among the ferns. Two nests of eggs.

**Narration:** "Crouch in the ferns and the raptors lose you. Stand up, and they come."

---

## 6 — CANYON PASS · 0:25–0:29

**Visual:** A narrow rock corridor, sheer layered walls close on both sides, loose scree and
boulders underfoot, a thin strip of sky above. Camera moves through at walking height.

**Narration:** "Cross the swamp, the nesting grounds, the canyon and the lava fields."

---

## 7 — ASH FOREST · 0:29–0:33

**Visual:** A dead forest under falling ash. Burnt black trunks, charred fallen logs, drifts of
grey ash over the ground, thick colourless haze, no undergrowth. Bleak and still.

**Narration:** *(continues over)*

---

## 8 — LAVA FIELDS · 0:33–0:39

**Visual:** A plain of cracked black basalt, orange lava glowing in the seams between the
plates, jagged basalt spires rising out of it, steam venting. A volcano smoking on the skyline
under an orange sky. Bleached bones on the crust.

**Narration:** "Find the key artifact. It opens the portal home."

---

## 9 — BOSS ARENA · 0:39–0:44.5

**Visual:** A wide arena ringed by obsidian shards and basalt, a tall stone portal ring standing
at the far end lit from within. A twelve-metre tyrannosaur turns toward camera. Heat haze, ash
in the air.

**Narration:** "Rex Prime guards it. Wounding him only makes him faster."

---

## Narration only — for a single voice take

> Chronoshift.
>
> A time traveller, stranded a hundred million years before his own.
>
> Era One. The prehistoric age.
>
> You arrive with nothing. You survive with a spear, a club, a sling and a bola.
>
> Crouch in the ferns and the raptors lose you. Stand up, and they come.
>
> Cross the swamp, the nesting grounds, the canyon and the lava fields.
>
> Find the key artifact. It opens the portal home.
>
> Rex Prime guards it. Wounding him only makes him faster.

**Delivery:** male, low and unhurried, roughly 8% slower than conversational. Weight on the
nouns — *spear, club, sling, bola* — not on the verbs. No rising inflection at line ends.

**Style keywords for Flow:** cinematic game trailer, late Cretaceous, volumetric god rays,
handheld drift, shallow depth of field, warm low sun, 24 fps motion blur, no on-screen text.
