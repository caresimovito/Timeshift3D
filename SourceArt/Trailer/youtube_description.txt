CHRONOSHIFT — Era One: The Prehistoric Age | First Level Trailer

A time traveller is stranded a hundred million years before his own.

Era One of sixteen.

Era One drops you into the late Cretaceous with nothing. You survive with what you can cut,
carve and sharpen — a spear, a bone club, a sling and a bola. Crouch in the ferns and the
raptors lose you; stand up and they come. Cross the jungle, the swamp, the nesting grounds,
the canyon pass, the ash forest and the lava fields to find the key artifact that opens the
portal home. Rex Prime guards it, and wounding him only makes him faster.

This is the first level, captured in-engine. The portal doesn't send you home -- it sends you
somewhere else. Sixteen eras are planned; this is the first.

— WHAT'S IN IT —

• Nine distinct areas, from jungle canopy to a plain of cracked basalt with lava in the seams
• Four primitive weapons, swapped on the fly
• Crouch-based stealth that genuinely changes whether you're seen
• Seven dinosaur species, each with their own animations and reactions
• A two-phase boss who speeds up as he takes damage
• Environmental hazards — lava, tar, volcanic vents

— MADE WITH —

Unreal Engine 5.8. Terrain sculpted from generated heightmaps, foliage placed with PCG,
creatures modelled and rigged in Blender. All footage is rendered from the running game, not
a cinematic mock-up.
