# Chronoshift — trailer copy (Prehistoric Era)

## YouTube description

**CHRONOSHIFT — Prehistoric Era Trailer**

The jump was meant to last seconds. It lasted sixty-six million years.

Stranded at the wrong end of history with a failing temporal rig, you have one
route home, and it runs forward. Survive the age of dinosaurs, log what hunts
you, and climb back up the timeline one era at a time.

Your tracker already holds entries you haven't earned yet — creatures from
epochs you have not reached. The Cretaceous is where you land. It is nowhere
near where this ends.

— Survive an open prehistoric world where everything larger than you has an
  opinion about it
— Scavenge and wield what the era allows: spear, bone club, torch, stone
— Stay unseen. Crouch, break line of sight, and let the herd tell you what it
  has noticed before you do
— Catalogue 20 species in the Temporal Log, from Compsognathus swarms that kill
  by arithmetic to a tyrannosaur that owns its own ground
— Every era you survive drags the rig one step closer to home

Built in Unreal Engine 5.

More eras in development. Follow for the next jump.

The copy sticks to what is actually in the build: stealth and detection, the
spear / bone club / torch / stone weapons, the 20-entry Temporal Log, the
tyrannosaur arena fight. It does not claim crafting, multiplayer or a release
date. If any of those land later, the description is the first thing to update.

### Hashtag guidance for X

Two or three, no more — X ranks dense tag lists as spam and they eat characters
that the hook needs. Pick one identity tag, one audience tag, one tech tag:

| Slot | Options |
|---|---|
| identity | `#Chronoshift` (always) |
| audience | `#IndieDev` `#IndieGame` `#Dinosaurs` `#GameDev` |
| tech | `#UE5` `#UnrealEngine5` |

`#IndieDev` reaches other developers; `#Dinosaurs` reaches players. If the goal
is wishlists rather than dev-community reach, use `#Dinosaurs`.

Best posting windows for #IndieDev reach are the Wednesday and Friday
screenshot-share hours, when the tag is already being browsed.
